Pick the photos for the expo booklet

  1. Open this in Chrome on a laptop. Set Editing as at the top to your name.
  2. Every grey dashed box is a gap for a photo. Drag a photo from your computer onto it, or click the box and choose Photo library in the panel on the right for our GHL photos.
  3. Double-click any words to change them. Drag anything to move it, pull the corner dots to resize.
  4. Thoughts for each other go on a Sticky note. Nikki's are purple , Liam's are green . Notes never print.
  5. Add, copy, move or delete pages with the Pages buttons. They work on the page with the honey outline.
  6. The last page is the Shot list for Jake's shoot. Write down any still you want and which page it is for. It never prints.
  7. When you are done, click Save new version and send the downloaded file back to Nikki.
